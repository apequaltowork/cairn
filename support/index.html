apequaltowork@gmail.com

Bug reports, feature requests, and general questions all welcome. Include your Chrome version and, for bugs, the steps that led to it if you can.

Before you email — quick fixes

  • A tab didn't restore. Chrome blocks extensions from reopening its own internal pages (chrome://), and some URLs expire or break over time. Cairn skips these rather than failing, and tells you how many were skipped.
  • Keyboard shortcut doesn't work. Another extension may have claimed it — go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts and reassign.
  • Old-tab detection seems off. Chrome doesn't expose exactly when a tab was opened, so Cairn uses the most recent time you viewed it, falling back to browsing history. Tabs restored from a previous browser session may show unusual ages.
  • Lost your workspaces. If Chrome's data for the extension was cleared, or it was uninstalled and reinstalled, local storage is wiped — this is why Settings → Export data is worth doing occasionally as a backup.
